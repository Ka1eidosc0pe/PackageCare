# Product design direction

Visual thesis: a calm, warm-white evidence workspace with deep teal actions, strong editorial typography, and generous, aligned whitespace.

Content plan: persistent product navigation; delivery list; dominant video and evidence timeline; recipient inspector; report preview. No marketing hero or dashboard card mosaic.

Interaction thesis: delivery selection crossfades the evidence workspace; timeline selection moves the playhead and reveals its evidence; the report drawer enters from the right and preserves review context. Respect reduced motion.

The UI makes footage provenance, uncertainty, unsaved changes, and recipient/model distinctions visible. Fixture mode is labeled across the workspace and exports.
